Qualifier Bet

Place a bet to spin the qualifier slot. If you match 3 coins, you enter the Coin Flip bonus where you pick a side.

How the bonus pays

If you qualify, both sides of the coin show random multipliers (2×–100×). Pick the side you think will land and the coin flips. Win = your bet × that side's multiplier. The Top Slot above the coin can occasionally boost one side's multiplier up to 100×.
