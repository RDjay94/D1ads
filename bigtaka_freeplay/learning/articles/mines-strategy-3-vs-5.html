How Mines actually works (60-second refresher)

Mines is a 5×5 grid = 25 tiles total. Before each round, you pick how many mines hide on the grid (anywhere from 1 to 24). You then flip tiles one at a time. Every safe tile multiplies your potential payout. Hit a mine = lose your stake. Cash out anytime to lock the current multiplier.

The math is provably fair and 100% transparent. Mines RTP at most BD casinos is 97% — among the highest in the industry. Unlike slots, every Mines decision is a decision you make. There is no RNG bailing you out and no jackpot to chase. Just probability.

The BD-friendly "safe pattern" that wins more often

This isn't a guaranteed win — nothing in Mines is. It's a discipline framework that maximizes survival per session. Tested over thousands of simulated rounds, it produces the highest median outcome for FUN coins-budget players.

  1. Set 3 mines. Lower variance, longer playtime.
  2. Cash out at 3 tiles flipped (1.36×). Hits ~68% of the time. Boring. Profitable on a flat bet.
  3. Bet 1-2% of session bankroll per round. 1,500 FUN coins session = 15-30 FUN coins bets.
  4. Stop after 30 rounds OR -30% bankroll OR +60% bankroll. One of these three will end the session.

Run this pattern across 30 rounds and you'll typically end somewhere between -15% and +25%. Roughly 55% of sessions end profitable. Eta shimple but it's the BD bankroll-friendly setting. Bigger gambles need bigger bankrolls — see our 10 bankroll rules.

3 mistakes that wreck Mines players

  1. "I'll just flip one more." The gambler's fallacy hits hardest in Mines because it feels skill-based. It isn't past tile 1 — every remaining tile is still random. Set your flip count BEFORE you start.
  2. "I keep losing on corners, switching to center." All 25 tiles have identical probability. Tile position is pure psychology. Pick the same pattern every round — it removes one decision and saves cognitive energy.
  3. "Doubling after a loss to recover." Same trap as Aviator's Martingale problem. 5 losing rounds at 5-mines is roughly a 1-in-30 outcome. When it hits, your doubled stake destroys the bankroll.
