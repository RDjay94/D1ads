How Teen Patti works (online live version)

Teen Patti — literally "three cards" — is a 3-card poker variant. In live casino Teen Patti, you're not playing against other players. You're betting on which of two pre-dealt hands wins: Player A or Player B.

The flow:

  1. Dealer deals 3 cards face-down to Player A and 3 cards face-down to Player B.
  2. You bet on which side will have the higher hand, with optional side bets on top.
  3. Cards are revealed. Best 3-card poker hand wins.

Hand rankings (high to low): Trail (three of a kind) → Pure Sequence (straight flush) → Sequence (straight) → Color (flush) → Pair → High Card.

Main bet payouts

  • Player A wins — pays around 0.96:1 (after commission)
  • Player B wins — pays around 0.96:1 (after commission)
  • Tie — pays around 25:1 (rare but real)

How Andar Bahar works

Andar Bahar — literally "inside-outside" — is even simpler. The dealer reveals one "joker" card. You bet on which side (Andar = left, Bahar = right) will draw a card matching the joker's value first. The dealer then alternately deals cards to Andar and Bahar until a match appears.

The flow:

  1. Dealer flips the joker card (the target).
  2. You bet on Andar (left), Bahar (right), or side bets.
  3. Dealer deals cards one by one to each side until a match.
  4. The side that gets the match first wins.

Main bet payouts

  • Andar (joker is black) — pays 0.9:1 if Andar wins (51.5% chance)
  • Bahar (joker is black) — pays 1:1 if Bahar wins (48.5% chance)
  • Payouts flip when the joker color flips. Whichever side gets the first card is the "favored" side.

Speed, pace & session length

This matters more than most BD players realize. The faster the game, the more bets per hour, the more your edge-disadvantage stacks. Here's the rough timing per round:

  • Andar Bahar: 30-50 seconds per round, depending on how many cards before the match. Roughly 70-90 rounds per hour.
  • Teen Patti: 50-70 seconds per round — slower because of card reveal animations and side-bet resolution. Roughly 50-65 rounds per hour.

So in a 1-hour session betting 50 taka per round:

  • Andar Bahar: 80 rounds × 50tk × 2.15% edge = around 86 taka expected loss
  • Teen Patti: 60 rounds × 50tk × 3.10% edge = around 93 taka expected loss

Pretty close. Teen Patti's bigger edge is offset by its slower pace. The real difference is the experience, not the math.

The side-bet question

Both games offer side bets. Teen Patti has the more interesting menu — Pair Plus (any pair or better in either hand pays up to 100:1), and 3-card combinations paying up to 1000:1. Andar Bahar offers a "1st card" side bet (which side gets the first match) and joker-value bets.

Here's the catch. Side bets have edges between 6% and 12%. They're built to look exciting on the felt and they sometimes pay big — but over a 100-round session, your "side bet bankroll" will leak roughly 3× faster than your main bet. Treat them as occasional flutters, not strategy.

The smart side-bet rule

If you're going to play side bets, allocate no more than 10% of your session bankroll to them. Place them on rounds where you already have a strong main bet — never as a standalone wager.
