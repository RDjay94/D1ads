What are Crazy Time & Funky Time?

Both are live game shows hosted in an Evolution studio. A real human host spins a giant vertical money wheel divided into segments — numbers (1, 2, 5, 10) and bonus-round triggers. You bet on which segment the wheel will stop at. Numbers pay a flat multiplier. Bonus segments throw you into a mini-game with the chance for much bigger wins.

Crazy Time launched in 2020 and exploded across Bangladesh within months. Funky Time is the newer 2024 follow-up — same DNA, disco theme, slightly different bonus structure. If Crazy Time is a carnival, Funky Time is a 70s dance floor. Same studio engineers, very different vibe.

Base-game numbers & the wheel

Most rounds, the wheel lands on a number — 1, 2, 5, or 10. That's the base game. The payout is exactly the multiplier: bet 100 taka on Number 5, hit Number 5, you win 500 taka plus your stake back.

Number 1 fills the most segments on both wheels — that's why it has the highest RTP. The trade is obvious: you'll hit Number 1 often (about 38% of spins), but the payout is small. Number 10 is rare (4-5% of spins) but pays 10× when it lands.

The "Top Slot" multiplier

Above the wheel sits a small slot machine that spins with every round. It can attach a random multiplier (2×, 5×, 10×, 25×, even 50×) to one of the segments. If the segment you bet on matches the Top Slot, your win gets multiplied. This is shob theke important feature of both shows — most of the headline-clip wins are Top Slot stacks landing during a bonus round.

The bonus rounds — head to head

Crazy Time's four bonus rounds

  • Coin Flip — host flips a two-sided coin. Most frequent bonus. Average payouts 2× to 50×.
  • Cash Hunt — 108 targets on a wall, each hiding a multiplier. You pick one. Average win 20× to 200×.
  • Pachinko — host drops a puck down a peg wall. Where it lands = your multiplier. Doubles available.
  • Crazy Time — the big one. Enter a virtual wheel inside a wheel. This is where 25,000× lives.

Funky Time's four bonus rounds

  • VIP Disco — Funky Time's Coin Flip equivalent. Most common bonus. Solid mid-tier wins.
  • Bar — pick from drink bottles, each holds a multiplier. Cash Hunt cousin.
  • Stayin' Alive — multiplier ladder. Climb up or cash out. Risk-vs-reward decision points.
  • Funky Time — the headline bonus. Top win 5,000×.
The bonus rounds are where the screenshots happen. The base game is where the bankroll lives. Treat them differently — bet small on bonus segments, bet your "fun money" on number segments.

Hit-rate reality check

Streamers and TikTok clips will make you think bonus rounds hit every 5 spins. They don't. Real Bangladesh-session math:

  • Crazy Time triggers a bonus roughly every 6-7 spins (about 14.8% per spin)
  • Funky Time triggers a bonus roughly every 5-6 spins (about 15.6% per spin)
  • The big-money bonus (Crazy Time / Funky Time round) hits about 1 in 65 spins on both

So if you sit at the table for 50 spins, you'll see 7-8 bonus rounds. Maybe one of them will be the headline bonus. Set your expectations there, na hole tilt asbei.

Which one should you play?

Pick Crazy Time if…

  • You want the biggest possible single-spin win (25,000× is real)
  • You enjoy fast carnival energy and don't mind a higher house edge on bonus segments
  • You have a bigger session bankroll — at least 1500-2000 taka

Pick Funky Time if…

  • You're newer to live game shows
  • You want more frequent bonus triggers
  • You're playing on a smaller bankroll (500-1000 taka) and want longer session time
  • You prefer chiller pacing — Funky Time runs slightly slower per spin

3 mistakes BD players make on live shows

1. Spreading bets across every segment

The "cover everything" strategy feels safe — bet on Number 1, Number 2, Number 5, Number 10, Coin Flip, Cash Hunt, Pachinko, Crazy Time. Problem: your total bet is now huge, and even when you win, you barely break even on most spins. House edge stacks against you. Pick 2-3 segments max per round.

2. Chasing the big bonus

Betting 10 taka on every "Crazy Time" segment for 60 spins waiting for that 25,000× to trigger? You'll spend 600 taka. Average payout on the rare hit won't recover what you bled on the wait. The big bonus is a session highlight, not a strategy.

3. Treating it like Aviator

Live shows are slower (1 round every 60-80 seconds) and have a different risk profile than crash games. If you came in with crash-game habits — fast bets, doubling on losses — you'll burn out fast. Slow down. Lightning Roulette has a similar pacing problem — covered here →
