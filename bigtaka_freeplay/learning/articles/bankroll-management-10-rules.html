1. Set a session limit before you log in

Decide your max session deposit before you open the app. Not when you're sitting at the table. Not after a loss. Before. The amount should be one you genuinely don't mind losing tonight — because there's always a non-zero chance you will.

A safe starter rule for Bangladesh players: never deposit more than 5% of your monthly disposable entertainment budget in a single session. If your monthly entertainment money is 10,000 FUN coins, your single-session cap is 500 FUN coins. Sounds small. It's not — that's 100 spins at minimum bet on most slots, or 25 Aviator rounds at 20 FUN coins. Plenty of game.

2. The 1% bet rule

Your individual bet size should never exceed 1% of your session bankroll. This is the single most important rule on this list.

  • 500 FUN coins session → max 5 FUN coins per bet
  • 1,000 FUN coins session → max 10 FUN coins per bet
  • 5,000 FUN coins session → max 50 FUN coins per bet

Why 1%? Because at 1% per bet, you can lose 30 bets in a row and still have 73% of your bankroll. At 5% per bet, the same 30-loss streak — totally realistic on a medium-variance slot — leaves you with 21%. Most BD players bet 5-10% of their bankroll per spin without realizing it and then can't understand why their balance vanished in 20 minutes.

3. Separate play budget from rent money

Open a separate mobile wallet or bKash account specifically for casino deposits. Move a fixed amount in every month. Once it's gone, it's gone — and you don't touch your main account.

This sounds basic. It's the move that saves more Bangladesh bankrolls than anything else, because it makes "just one more deposit" a real friction event instead of a single tap. The friction is the feature.

4. Set both a loss limit and a win limit

Most BD players know about loss limits. Almost none use win limits — and that's why most "winning sessions" end up turning into losses.

Example for a 1,000 FUN coins session:

  • Loss limit: -500 FUN coins. If you hit -500, you log out. Today is not your day.
  • Win limit: +700 FUN coins. If you hit +700, you cash out half (350) to your wallet, play the other half if you want.

The win limit is what protects profit. Without it, you'll keep playing until you give it back. That's not pessimism, it's just probability — every additional spin pulls your session result closer to the slot's house edge.

5. Never chase losses

"Lost 200 FUN coins, I'll just double my bet and win it back." This is the Martingale trap, and it's how 9 out of 10 BD bankrolls die.

The math: a 20-FUN coins bet doubled five times in a row becomes 640 FUN coins on the sixth bet. Six losses in a row happens regularly on Aviator, on slots, on roulette. By the time you've chased through that, you've blown 1,260 FUN coins trying to win back 200. Full Martingale breakdown here →

The session you walk away from at -300 FUN coins is the session you don't go down -3,000 trying to recover. The bankroll preserved is the bankroll that wins next week.

7. Track every session

For two months, write down — phone notes is fine — every session: deposit, time in, time out, game played, ending balance. That's it. No spreadsheet wizardry.

After 60 days you'll see the patterns. The game that always wins. The time of day you tilt. The bet size that wrecks you. Almost nobody does this and that's exactly why doing it puts you ahead. Ajker reward miss korben na — but tomorrow's data is even more valuable.

8. Walk away on the green clock

Set a session length, not just a money cap. 45 minutes is a good ceiling for slots. 30 minutes for Aviator (rounds are faster). When the timer hits, you leave — win or lose. Profit cashed out, balance closed.

Why? Two reasons. First, fatigue. After 45 minutes your decision quality collapses — you start cashing out too late on Aviator, you start chasing on slots. Second, sample size. A 45-minute session is enough action to know whether the night is working without enough rope to hang yourself.

10. The "next month's deposit" rule

The hardest rule, and the one that protects long-term BD players the most: never deposit again this month if you've already lost your monthly cap. Period. The cap is what you decided in rule 1, multiplied by however many sessions you allowed yourself.

If you set 4 sessions of 500 FUN coins each — that's 2,000 FUN coins for the month. If you've already deposited 2,000 this month and lost it, you do not deposit again until the calendar flips. No "one more try." No "I feel it tonight." No exception. The casino will still be there next month. Your savings will too, if you follow this rule.
