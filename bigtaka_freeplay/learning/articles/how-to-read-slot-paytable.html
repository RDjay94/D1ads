Where to find the paytable

Almost every online slot hides the paytable behind the same three icons: the "i" info button, the three-line menu, or sometimes a small book icon. You'll find it in the bottom-left or bottom-right of the screen on mobile, or top-right on desktop. Tap once and it opens like a small in-game manual.

Don't skip this. Spending 60 seconds in the paytable before your first real-money spin is the single highest-ROI thing a Bangladesh player can do. Free, painless, and you'll instantly stop playing slots you have no business being on.

1. RTP — the most important number

RTP (Return to Player) is the percentage of all wagered money a slot is designed to pay back over the long run. Anything 96% or higher is solid. 95% is okay. Below 94% is a red flag — you're paying a 6%+ house edge per spin, which adds up fast.

  • 97%+ RTP: excellent. Examples: Blood Suckers (98%), 1429 Uncharted Seas (98.6%).
  • 96-97% RTP: the industry standard. Most popular slots in BD sit here — Sweet Bonanza (96.5%), Gates of Olympus (96.5%).
  • 94-96% RTP: acceptable but not great. Look for the same theme at a better RTP.
  • Below 94%: avoid. Some slots ship multiple RTP versions — operators pick the cheapest. Always check.

Quick rule for BD players: if the paytable doesn't show the RTP at all, that's a warning sign. Skip the slot. Full RTP guide here →

2. Max win and hit rate

The max win is the biggest possible payout the slot can produce on a single spin or feature, usually expressed as a multiplier of your bet (e.g. 5,000× bet). The hit rate (sometimes called hit frequency) is how often any winning combination lands.

These two numbers together tell you the slot's variance at a glance:

Max WinHit RateWhat It Means
500-1,000× bet30%+Low variance — small steady wins
1,000-5,000× bet22-30%Medium variance — balanced
5,000-50,000× bet15-22%High variance — rare big hits

3. Paylines and ways-to-win

Old-school slots paid only on specific lines — usually 9, 20, or 25 paylines running across the reels. Modern slots often use ways-to-win mechanics (243 ways, 1,024 ways, even 117,649 ways on Megaways slots), where any matching symbols on adjacent reels pay.

  • Paylines: symbols must land on a defined path. The paytable shows you each line shape.
  • Ways-to-win: direction doesn't matter — adjacent matching symbols win.
  • Cluster pays: 5+ matching symbols touching each other anywhere on the grid pay (Sweet Bonanza, Reactoonz).
  • Megaways: the number of symbols per reel changes each spin, so the number of ways changes too.

Why does this matter? More ways-to-win usually means smaller individual line wins but more frequent hits. The slot will still pay 96% over time — it's just distributed differently. Don't pick a slot based on payline count alone.

4. Symbol values and wilds

The biggest part of the paytable is usually the symbol value chart. Each symbol shows what 3, 4, or 5 of a kind pays — usually as a multiplier of your stake or your line bet.

What to actually look at:

  • The top symbol's 5-of-a-kind payout. If that's only 10× your bet, the slot's max win is going to come from features, not base game. If it's 100×+, base spins can pay big.
  • Wild symbol behavior. Does the wild only substitute? Or does it have a multiplier (2×, 3×, 5×)? Multiplier wilds are massive for high-variance slots.
  • Scatter payouts. Some scatters pay directly in addition to triggering bonuses. Free wins, basically.
The paytable is the only place that doesn't lie. The slot's UI can hype, the marketing can promise, the streamers can edit — but the paytable's numbers are the maths the RNG actually runs.

5. Bonus and free-spin triggers

This is where 80% of a high-variance slot's max win lives. The paytable will tell you exactly how to trigger them and what they do.

Things to check:

  • How many scatters trigger free spins? 3 is standard, 4 is rarer, 5 is rare. Slots that need 5 scatters for free spins almost always have higher base-game hits to compensate.
  • How many free spins do you get? 8-10 = standard. 15+ = generous.
  • Multipliers in free spins? Sticky wilds, persistent multipliers, retriggers — these features turn 10 free spins into the actual big-win mode.
  • Buy-feature price. If the slot lets you buy free spins (Sweet Bonanza, Gates of Olympus), the paytable shows the cost. Usually 100× your bet. Don't buy bonuses if your bankroll is small — it's a 100-bet single-shot bet. Bankroll rules apply →

The 60-second checklist

Before any new slot, scan the paytable for these six things in order. Takes under a minute once it's habit. এখানে strategy ta simple:

  1. RTP — must be 96%+ ideally.
  2. Max win — multiplier of bet. Matches your variance preference?
  3. Hit rate — if not listed, infer from max win.
  4. Wild behavior — does it multiply? Stick? Expand?
  5. Bonus trigger — 3 scatters, 4, 5? How often statistically?
  6. Buy feature — available? Worth it for your bankroll?

You don't need to memorize a slot's whole paytable. You just need to know whether this slot is worth your taka tonight, or whether you should pick the next one. 60 seconds. Every new slot. That's the whole habit.
